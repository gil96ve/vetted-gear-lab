## Paso 1 — 10 pain points

1. Tire pressure drops in cold weather and no gauge is within reach.
2. The gas-station air pump is broken, coin-only, or closed for the night.
3. A portable inflator cannot start a dead battery.
4. A jump starter cannot fix an underinflated tire.
5. Drivers confuse 4000 peak amps with sustained cold-cranking power.
6. Over-inflating a tire is easy without a clear auto shutoff.
7. A small compressor heat-soaks during the second or third tire.
8. The clamps or the air chuck are missing from the trunk when needed.
9. A dead phone leaves no way to check pressure or call for help.
10. Trunk space is too tight for two separate roadside tools.
