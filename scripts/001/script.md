## Paso 1 — 10 pain points

1. A 12V battery dies in winter and there are no jumper cables in the trunk.
2. No second car is available to give a jump.
3. Conventional cables are too short or the terminals are hard to reach.
4. Buyers confuse "peak amps" with sustained cranking performance.
5. Connecting clamps backwards can damage vehicle electronics.
6. A compact pack is assumed to work on diesel engines it cannot start.
7. A pack is dead after a few uses because capacity was never checked.
8. Rain, snow, or dust reaches the unit during a roadside emergency.
9. The engine bay is too dark to see terminals at night.
10. A phone battery dies while the driver is stranded and needs help.
